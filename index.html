Car Cricket — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Car Cricket ("the app") is a vehicle-spotting cricket scoring game. This page explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information stored on your device

Player names, game settings, match history, and statistics you enter are stored locally on your device only. This information is never transmitted to us or to any server we control, and we have no access to it.

In-app purchases

The app offers an optional one-time "Pro" purchase to unlock additional features and remove ads. Purchases are processed through Google Play's billing system and managed using RevenueCat, a third-party subscription management service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-generated user identifier and purchase/transaction data in order to verify your purchase and keep your unlocked features in sync. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Advertising

Free-tier use of the app displays banner advertisements served through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers (such as your Android Advertising ID) to serve and measure ads. Google's use of this information is governed by Google's own privacy policy and ad policies: policies.google.com/privacy and google.com/about/company/user-consent-policy. Purchasing the Pro upgrade removes ads and stops this data collection going forward.

What we do not do

  • We do not require you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or contact details.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

Children's privacy

Car Cricket is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to chief@treeware.co.za.
